Lifecycle Architecture

From first signal to accepted pipeline

A working model for connecting acquisition source, contact progression, meeting outcomes, opportunity acceptance, forecasting, and post-sale handoff.

A lifecycle map is only useful when it defines what each state means, what causes a record to move, and which object owns the truth. This example separates contact progression from meeting disposition and pipeline acceptance, so activity does not automatically become forecastable pipeline.

A lead generated through a website, content, event, or referral. It enters the same qualification framework as every other new lead.

Partner

A lead referred by a channel partner. Worked like any other lead until it qualifies.

Alternate entry path

Partner Registration

A qualified partner referral can enter near the qualification gate without being forced through every top-of-funnel status.

Design principle

  • Preserve the original source
  • Apply the same acceptance criteria
  • Create the Stage 0 opportunity only when the meeting is scheduled

Contact Status

New

A net-new contact that has not yet been evaluated or worked.

Entry and activities

  • Capture source, account, and essential firmographic context
  • Run enrichment, validation, and ownership rules
  • Determine whether the record is actionable

Exit criteria

  • Meets the sales-ready threshold
  • Otherwise routes to Nurture or Archived

Contact Status

Sales Ready

A source-agnostic quality gate indicating that the contact is ready for sales engagement.

Entry and activities

  • Meets agreed fit and signal criteria
  • Routes to the appropriate owner with relevant context

Exit criteria

  • A seller accepts and begins working the record
  • If not accepted, the record leaves the active queue

Lifecycle status should represent business meaning, not simply a technical side effect of record creation or conversion. This separates system automation from the qualification decision.

Contact Status

Working

A seller has accepted ownership and is actively working the contact.

Typical work

  • Personalized outreach across appropriate channels
  • Initial qualification and context gathering
  • Qualification meeting scheduled when interest is confirmed

Exit criteria

  • Two-way engagement or a scheduled qualification meeting

Contact Status

Engaged

A meaningful two-way interaction has been reached.

Exit criteria

  • A qualification meeting is scheduled

Opportunity Stage · acceptance gate

Qualification Review

A pre-pipeline opportunity created when a qualification meeting is scheduled. It captures demand without inflating accepted pipeline before the meeting occurs and the opportunity is validated.

Exit criteria

Opportunity Stage

Unqualified

A Stage 0 opportunity that did not meet pipeline-acceptance criteria. Only reachable from Stage 0.

0%Probability
OmittedForecast

Reasons

  • No validated need
  • Outside supported use cases or ICP
  • No meaningful engagement
  • No credible next step
  • Timeline beyond the window
  • Duplicate or invalid

Off-ramp

Nurture

Not actively worked. The contact can re-enter the working funnel when new evidence makes it actionable.

Design principle

  • Available from any Contact Status
  • Preserves original source and lifecycle history
  • Re-entry requires a new signal or intentional review

Off-ramp

Archived

A disqualified, duplicate, invalid, or otherwise unusable contact.

Design principle

  • Available from any Contact Status
  • Requires a structured reason
  • Retains history without leaving the record in an active queue

How the meeting links Contact and Opportunity

The qualification meeting is tracked separately from the opportunity it belongs to. One Stage 0 opportunity is created with the first booking and can accumulate additional meeting records over time.

ContactMeeting Scheduled books → Qualification meetingthe scheduled interaction opens → OpportunityStage 0
CardinalityMany contacts can map to one opportunity. One opportunity can hold many meetings; each reschedule, no-show, or re-book becomes a distinct meeting record.
Each meeting carriesA disposition describing whether it happened and, only if it occurred, an opportunity outcome: Accept, Hold, or Disqualify.
