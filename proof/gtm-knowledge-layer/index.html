Selected work / Sales knowledge

Making sales knowledge availablewhere sellers work.

Getting an answer was work in itself. Reps had to search across documentation, work out what was current, or ask a colleague in Slack. The information often existed, but using it depended on knowing where to look or whom to ask.

That interrupted both the seller trying to move forward and the colleague helping them. The same questions kept coming back.

What I built / Lumana

A shared reference. A way to ask across it.

Adoption and feedback / Buildkite

Helping the team use it and improve it.

At Buildkite, I owned the rollout of a separate sales knowledge implementation. I interviewed reps and stakeholders, then led training, an adoption challenge and feedback loops. Their questions helped identify missing or unclear guidance and strengthened the connection between Product Marketing and Sales.

The impact on the team

Less back-and-forth. Less dependence on a colleague.

Sellers could resolve more questions themselves, reducing the Slack exchanges needed to locate information and explain it again. The people supporting them had fewer repeat questions to field. At Buildkite, feedback also helped improve the shared guidance, so what the team learned could help the next person.
