Selected work / Sales knowledge

Making sales knowledgeusable for the team.

Reps had to search separate documentation spaces or find someone who knew the answer. Some of the product information they needed was in an engineering repository they could not access.

What was getting in the way

Sales processes, onboarding material and technical guidance had accumulated in different places, without a clear rule about which source to trust. Asking a colleague meant relying on what that person remembered. Adding an agent to that content would leave the same gaps and conflicting guidance underneath its answers.

How I approached it

I helped consolidate the sales knowledge base and defined which sources belonged, what should retire and who could maintain the material. I built the Sales Knowledge Agent across that broader knowledge base, along with the publishing workflows and source controls needed to support it.

What I did

Bring the knowledge together, then make it easier to use.

The work covered sales guidance, product documentation and technical references, with the agent built across those sources.

01

Establish where sellers should find guidance.

Consolidation, content ownership and access

The problem

A sales knowledge base, onboarding space, legacy sales space and cold-call grading space all held material. Product documentation was missing from the places sellers could read.

My work

I helped consolidate the sales knowledge base, decided which spaces should remain or retire, and set contribution and editing rules. The scope included sales processes, onboarding, stage definitions, buying signals and selected product and technical guidance.

I also built a publisher to bring 237 product-documentation pages into the sales workspace and synchronize source changes daily.

What changed

Sellers gained a shared reference for sales guidance and access to product documentation that had previously been out of reach.

02

Build an agent across the sales knowledge base.

Finding answers and preserving the detail behind them

The problem

Making documentation available still left a rep needing to know where to look. Product answers also needed to retain the conditions and limitations that could get lost in a summary.

My work

I built the Sales Knowledge Agent to answer from the approved sources and point back to the supporting pages. I wrote its instructions to explain when the material did not answer a question rather than fill the gap.

I added a product-documentation specialist instructed to quote the source, reviewed the technical specialist’s references and corrected a conflicting instruction that had blocked product questions.

What changed

Reps could ask across the sales knowledge base as well as read it directly, with instructions for citations, product qualifications and missing answers built into the agent.

03

Maintain what the team would be relying on.

Source review, publishing and editing permissions

The problem

Internal engineering material was not automatically suitable for a sales answer. Broad editing permissions could change the agent’s sources, while repeated publishing could overwrite pages and obscure meaningful changes.

My work

I removed a technical source describing an unsupported configuration and added disclosure instructions for the remaining material. I tightened knowledge-base editing rights and made the published documentation space view-and-comment only, verifying permissions through the API.

The publisher used full page paths to prevent naming collisions, avoided unresolved links and skipped unchanged pages. I extended publication to the partner portal through an explicit partner-facing label and rebuilt the support knowledge base from the same product-documentation source.

What changed

The knowledge had defined editing and audience rules. Product updates could serve sales, partners and support while unchanged pages retained their useful history.

The results

Sellers could get to the knowledge.
The business could maintain it.

A knowledge base and an agent

Scattered sales guidance was brought together, with a seller-facing agent built across approved sources and specialist instructions for product and technical questions.

237 pages made accessible

Product documentation became available in the sales workspace, with changes synchronized daily. The same documentation source supported sales, partner and support content.

Useful history, less rewriting

A publishing run with no source changes went from rewriting all 237 pages in about eighteen minutes to writing nothing in about three.
