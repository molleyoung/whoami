Selected work / Sales knowledge

Making sales knowledgeusable for the team.

Reps had to search separate documentation spaces or find someone who knew the answer. Some of the product information they needed was in an engineering repository they could not access.

What was getting in the way

Sales processes, onboarding material and technical guidance had accumulated in different places, without a clear rule about which source to trust. Asking a colleague meant relying on what that person remembered. Adding an agent to that content would leave the same gaps and conflicting guidance underneath its answers.

How I approached it

I helped consolidate the sales knowledge base and defined which sources belonged, what should retire and who could maintain the material. I built the Sales Knowledge Agent across that broader knowledge base, along with the publishing workflows and source controls needed to support it.

What I built

A sales knowledge base and an agent across it.

The problem

Content had accumulated across sales, onboarding, legacy and technical spaces. Availability alone did not establish which guidance to trust.

What I did

I helped consolidate the knowledge base and set decisions about what belonged, which sources should retire, who could contribute and edit, and how new agents would be created.

What changed

Sellers had a shared place to consult guidance, and maintainers had clearer responsibilities for its content.

The results

Sellers could get to the knowledge.
The business could maintain it.

For sellers

The team could consult shared sales guidance or ask the agent across approved sources, including product documentation that had previously been out of reach.

For partners and support

Approved content reached the partner portal through an explicit publishing label. Support documentation was rebuilt from the same product source used for sales.

For the people maintaining it

Daily publishing synchronized product changes, while unchanged pages kept their history. Defined editing rights and audience rules supported the ongoing work.
