Closed Won Review Agent

The problem

Closed Won did not mean a deal was ready for fulfillment.

As we connected Salesforce to NetSuite through Workato, incomplete or inconsistent deal data could move directly into fulfillment. Missing products or contracts, conflicting dates and terms, and the wrong customer, partner, billing entity, or address could delay fulfillment and create problems for revenue recognition or compensation.

Fixing the entire quote-to-cash workflow first would have slowed the integration and left the same downstream risk in place. We needed a practical control that could protect the handoff while the upstream process continued to improve.

The objective

Move the integration forward without passing known problems downstream.

The review needed to identify known issues, explain what had to be corrected, and give Sales, Finance, and RevOps a shared view of why a deal had not moved to fulfillment.

It also needed to preserve human judgment. The automation supports the review; people remain responsible for correcting the deal and approving it for release.

What I built

A human-in-the-loop review between Closed Won and fulfillment.

I built the process, checks, exception guidance, operating workflow, and Slack alerts that govern the handoff. A scheduled Python agent evaluates each eligible deal against the current business requirements. Deterministic checks handle explicit requirements, while Claude is used narrowly where interpretation or clearer remediation language is useful.

The result is not an autonomous approval. It is a consistent review that gives the responsible teams the information they need to correct issues, re-run the checks, and make the final decision.

How it works

Consistent checks, specific guidance, and a visible recheck loop.

The review separates repeatable business requirements from the cases that need interpretation. Instead of a generic rejection, the team receives specific guidance about what failed and why the deal cannot yet move to fulfillment.

01

02

Explicit checks

Known requirements are encoded as repeatable logic wherever possible.

03

Narrow AI use

Claude helps with limited interpretation and readable remediation guidance, not the approval decision.

Sales, Finance, and RevOps can see the result, the expectation, and the reason a deal is held.

The responsible team fixes the underlying data or supplies the missing context.

06

Recheck and release

Corrected deals return through the same review before a person approves the downstream handoff.

Improving the review

The control changes as the upstream workflow improves.

The review was designed as an iterative operating process, not a fixed list of checks. Alerts make recurring problems visible across Sales, Finance, and RevOps. That evidence helps distinguish systemic gaps from true exceptions and shows where an upstream process, field, automation, or enablement change will prevent the issue earlier.

As those changes are made, the review is updated. Checks can be narrowed, revised, or removed when the source workflow reliably prevents the problem.

Impact

A faster, more trusted handoff to fulfillment.

The process allowed the Salesforce-to-NetSuite integration to move forward without waiting for a full quote-to-cash redesign. It gave the teams a shared control for protecting fulfillment, revenue recognition, and compensation while upstream fixes were implemented.

Fulfillment had previously taken as long as a week in some cases. In the current process, deals move to fulfillment in about one day on average.
