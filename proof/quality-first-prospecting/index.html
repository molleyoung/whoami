GTM motion / flagship story

Quality-First Prospecting Engine

An end-to-end prospecting motion that turns signals into prioritized seller action, then uses outcomes and rep feedback to improve what happens next.

My role

Architecture · operating logic · workflow design · analysis · iteration

Starting condition

Signals, research, routing, and outreach existed. They did not operate as one connected motion.

Market signals, contact research, enrichment, account matching, ownership, and outreach lived in separate motions. That made it difficult to answer a basic operating question: which account and person should a rep work now, and why?

The deeper problem was feedback. Outcomes and rep observations were difficult to trace back to targeting, data quality, enrichment, phone-number quality, execution, or adoption.

Signal to Sequence

The architecture behind the experiment.

The engine carries context from a market signal through a governed decision and into the correct seller action. Outcomes then return to the system as evidence for the next build.

System detail

Every handoff preserves a reason to act.

The useful artifact is not a score in isolation. It is the connected operating system around it: data, history, ownership, execution, and measurement.

01

02

03

Use automation where it is appropriate and keep live calling and seller judgment visible.

06

Outcome + feedback

Connect response quality and seller observations to the specific population and build.

Quality-first experiment

A small signal became a measured operating test.

The working hypothesis was that a priority population selected with richer fit and signal context would connect at a higher rate than broad dialing. The earliest 17 to 32 call samples were directionally promising, but too small to treat as a durable result.

As volume increased, the result moderated. That change is part of the story: measurement replaced a promising snapshot with a more credible operating read.

Diagnosis

The score was one layer, not the whole answer.

When results moved, the operating question was where quality was leaking. The diagnosis covered the full system rather than assigning every outcome to prioritization.

01

Targeting

Was the population truly aligned to ICP and the triggering signal?

02

Data quality

Were identities, accounts, roles, and histories correct?

03

Enrichment

Did the build find the right people and enough context?

04

Phone quality

Were the numbers valid, current, and useful for dialing?

05

Execution

Did timing, messaging, cadence, and channel fit the moment?

06

Adoption

Was the priority population worked consistently and as designed?

Honest read
  • The earliest samples were provisional and should not be used as a headline result.
  • Performance moderated as the population scaled, which made the later read more useful.
  • Scoring alone did not materially change team-wide performance.
  • The durable accomplishment is the broader learning engine and its ability to keep evolving.
