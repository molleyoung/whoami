The problem

The process was getting in the way of the deal.

Partners couldn’t reliably register deals or get the guidance they needed.

Access required internal help. enablement materials were difficult to reach. Registration problems created work for both partners and the channel team.

Reps worked around pricing rules, then hit the wrong approvals.

When partner-tier pricing did not apply correctly, reps compensated with additional sales discounts. Those workarounds triggered inappropriate quote approvals and sent requests to the wrong reviewers.

How I approached it

Understand the work.
Then change the system.

I worked from leadership’s priorities through the daily workflows of channel managers, sales reps, and partners. I reviewed recent requests and deal questions, tested the systems myself, and mapped the integrations, records, and business handoffs.

I worked with enablement, technical writers, developers, Finance, Procurement, and our vendors to turn that understanding into a design we could implement. AI supported my investigation and build work throughout.

I kept the end state in view while prioritizing by business impact, effort, and dependencies. That meant covering immediate gaps, fixing foundations, and building capabilities the team could use along the way.

The impact

More partner self-service.
More reliable deals and channel reporting.

Partners can get access and find answers.

Partner administrators can invite and onboard their own team members directly. Courses, current guidance, AI search, and the copilot help partners find product and process information, with submission status available in the portal.

Teams can act on deals with better information.

Rebuilt registration automation, minimum data requirements, team views, and SLAs support action on submissions. Commercial controls and closed-won validation catch issues before they become downstream work.

Leadership can distinguish contribution and forecast the channel.

Business definitions and system logic separate sourced from influenced participation. The channel team can inspect conversion and performance and maintain a forecast separate from direct sales.

Mean closed-won approval time: about 2.6 days → under one day.
