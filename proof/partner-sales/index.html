Building the operationbehind partner sales.

I rebuilt the processes, partner experience, automation, and reporting behind partner sales so our teams could support the channel motion more effectively.

What was getting in the way

Partners struggled to access guidance and register deals. Reps worked around incorrect partner pricing and triggered the wrong approvals. Incorrect customer and partner details carried into quotes and orders, causing wrong invoices and slower fulfillment. Teams chased missing information across emails and DMs, while inconsistent records made channel performance difficult to trust.

How I approached it

I needed to understand where leadership wanted to take the channel and what was making that difficult for the team. I listened to reps and channel managers, reviewed the questions coming out of active deals, and worked through the systems myself to understand why people were making exceptions or working around the process.

From there, I defined what the channel needed, where it could share a process with direct sales, and where it needed a different one. I worked with the teams involved to prioritize changes by impact, effort, and dependencies, putting improvements into use while building toward the larger design.
