Selected work / Revenue Operations

Revenue Intelligence

The same question about revenue could produce a different answer depending on who pulled the report.

Teams used different definitions, fields had multiplied, and data hygiene was inconsistent. Leadership reporting and board preparation required extra reconciliation before we could present a consistent account of performance.

I worked across the reporting and its underlying systems: defining metrics, tracing calculations to source records, reconciling revenue, building dashboards and analysis, and delivering information where leaders, managers, and sellers could use it.

Why it mattered

It was harder to agree on priorities when we first had to agree on the numbers. Reconciling reports consumed time needed to investigate revenue gaps, decide where to intervene, and plan ahead.

What changed ↓How the work evolved ↓

Results

What changed

Investigating disagreements between reports exposed problems in the underlying definitions, records, and workflows. Fixing those causes changed what the business could see and act on.

Sales performance

A clear basis for win rate

I defined new-business win rate from accepted opportunity to win. The previous blended rate was roughly twice that figure. I also corrected a funnel that counted expansion opportunities at acceptance but excluded them from wins.

Revenue timing

Active revenue followed contract dates

Signed contracts that had not started were separated from active revenue. Reconciliation against contracts and Finance’s ledger also uncovered shipping charges inflating contract value and deals still contributing revenue after leaving Closed Won.

Investigation & action

Teams received findings they could act on

Win analysis connected closed deals with call recordings to identify buying use cases. Event conversion analysis provided evidence for marketing investment. Recurring analysis and written summaries brought findings to the team through Confluence and Slack.

Interactive reports exposed the underlying records for further investigation. Channel users could filter their own dashboards, while sellers and managers received priorities and pipeline updates.

Audit & history

Changes could be explained and traced back

Drift audits and alerts surfaced changes for investigation. Versioned reports and definitions, changelogs, and historical builds preserved what had been reported and how it was calculated, helping distinguish business movement from data corrections or calculation changes.

On the first weekend, a publication check caught a large increase in open pipeline. The increase was real, and the report went out with an explanation.

My contribution

Definitions, analysis, and the working software

Establish what the numbers mean

Build and run the reporting

Write data pulls, dashboard builds, analysis, validation, scheduled delivery, and failure handling. Maintain drift audits, alerts, changelogs, and versioned reports and definitions.

Build history

How the work evolved

Each iteration addressed a constraint in the previous experience. Definition work and reconciliation continued throughout, while different reporting surfaces served different needs.

01Rebuild the reporting leadership relied onTableau Cloud / audited Salesforce sources / daily refresh+

I rebuilt pipeline, SaaS metrics, and win-rate reporting in Tableau Cloud. Without Tableau Desktop, I generated data extracts in Python, wrote workbook definitions in code, and published through the API.

I traced fields back to Salesforce, added missing metrics, corrected calculations that could count a deal twice across periods, consolidated data sources, and automated the daily refresh.

What prompted the next iteration: cross-system questions and a slow publish-and-check cycle made it difficult to keep pace with the analysis the business needed.

Python · Tableau workbook definitions · Tableau API · Salesforce · shared extracts

02Answer questions across systemsInteractive HTML / source data / independent checks+

I began generating self-contained interactive dashboards in Python, with underlying data attached. Views covered win analysis, segments, product and revenue mix, activity, inbound, and channel.

  • Win analysis combined closed deals with call recordings and used AI to identify the use case behind each win.
  • Inbound analysis compared conversion across event types, providing evidence for budget decisions.
  • Activity comparisons used the same point in the week so a partial week did not appear to be a collapse.

I independently recomputed metrics before sharing them and established which source took precedence when reports disagreed. I withheld meetings-booked figures when the sources did not reconcile.

What prompted the next iteration: refreshes still depended on me running them. More reporting also made inconsistent definitions and source calculations increasingly visible.

Python · HTML / CSS / JavaScript · Salesforce · sales engagement · call recordings · Claude

03Automate delivery and investigate failuresScheduled builds / drift audits / version history+

I scheduled extraction, analysis, dashboard builds, publication, and stakeholder summaries. Recurring pipeline analysis combined statistical calculations with Claude-generated explanations, delivered through Confluence and Slack so the team received findings alongside the numbers.

Dashboards remained available through our documentation site, a domain-restricted web page, and verified shared-drive folders, with underlying data available for investigation.

Each build compared headline metrics with prior results and held publication when a movement needed investigation. I maintained drift audits and alerts, versioned reports and definitions, changelogs, and historical builds so I could trace what had been reported, how it was calculated, and what changed between versions.

That history made it possible to investigate whether a difference reflected business activity, corrected source data, or a calculation change, and to revisit the numbers used in earlier executive and board reporting.

When scheduled runs failed, I traced a cause to the machine sleeping mid-refresh, then added retries, freshness checks, and alerts identifying the failed stage.

What remained: reports still needed to reach people in their daily workflows. Scheduled execution also needed to move off a single machine.

Scheduled Python jobs · drift audits and alerts · changelogs · versioned reports and definitions · historical builds · Confluence · hosted dashboards · Google Drive

04Put information where people workSalesforce applications / seller priorities / manager summaries+

I rebuilt seller command centers as Salesforce app pages after the existing personalized-dashboard allocation limited expansion. I removed the target-account restriction so reps could see every account in their name and combined separate vendor signal views.

When standard components could not support the required signal counts, I built custom Lightning Web Components and an Apex back end enforcing record access, with automated test coverage. I maintained the application as version-controlled metadata and deployed through the Salesforce CLI.

Related workflows delivered ranked rep priorities, pipeline hygiene, quota attainment, manager summaries, and pending Closed Won reviews. Channel users gained native deal-registration dashboards they could filter and explore themselves.

What prompted further work: too many follow-up questions still depended on me, and cross-system interpretation needed consistent definitions available to both people and AI.

Salesforce app pages · Lightning Web Components · Apex · Salesforce CLI · Git · Python · Slack

05Make the business definitions available to AIGenerated documentation / executive pilot / connected data+

I built a reporting knowledge layer whose structured source generates both published documentation and AI instructions. It records field meanings, business definitions, and known data limitations.

I built a read-only executive pilot for plain-language Salesforce questions and validated its approved questions against live data. I also built a connected data layer bringing together nearly 952,000 records across CRM, sales engagement, call recording, marketing, and enrichment, checking extraction counts against their sources and standardizing terminology.

Source checks exposed a platform silently stopping its response after 10,000 records. Related knowledge tools include a governed sales knowledge agent and a dictionary explaining all 42 buying signals for reps.

Structured definitions · generated documentation and AI instructions · Claude · read-only Salesforce connection · local cross-system database
