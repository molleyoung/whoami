Design considerations

Build for leverage without losing what already works

Bringing businesses, data and systems together creates leverage, but the sequence matters. These are the considerations I would keep visible while the model takes shape.

Standardization

Earn the common model.

Validate where shared definitions, infrastructure and process create real leverage before consolidating motion-specific logic.

Business continuity

Preserve useful history and context.

Protect source, campaign, customer, lifecycle and pipeline history as definitions, architecture or systems change.

Team workflow

Make the operating model usable.

Design changes around the decisions and actions teams need to take, with the right context available at the right point in the workflow.

Automation + AI

Scale understood work.

Use automation and AI where the underlying decision, exception path and business outcome are clear enough to build against.

Measurement

Keep the business outcome in view.

Connect operational measures such as speed, reliability, capacity and data quality back to the revenue or business result the work is intended to improve.

Maintainability

Build something the business can own.

Document logic, governance, dependencies and failure paths so the environment remains understandable as the company and team evolve.
