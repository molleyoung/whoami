Where I could add value immediately

Start with the problems already in motion

A few of the problems from our conversations are already concrete enough to investigate while the broader operating model is being mapped.

Three motions → one useful revenue view

Create shared visibility without forcing one funnel.

Map how SPORT, EVENT and University Athlete create revenue, including their buying cycles and seasonality, then determine what leadership needs to see together and what should remain specific to each business.

  • Map each business from plan → seasonal demand → pipeline creation → sales motion → revenue.
  • Identify the definitions and customer context that should be shared.
  • Build a company-level leadership view above motion-specific reporting and forecasting.
HubSpot architecture + data model

Understand what the CRM needs to represent before redesigning it.

Work from the business model into lifecycle, pipeline, ownership, associations, source and reporting so the architecture reflects how the businesses need to operate.

  • Inspect company, contact and deal relationships across the businesses.
  • Identify duplicate logic, legacy definitions, broken handoffs and missing context.
  • Decide what should be standardized, migrated or remain different.
Growth + signal architecture

Connect signal, context and action.

Look at HubSpot, Apollo, Clay, LinkedIn Sales Navigator and web or campaign data as parts of a growth motion rather than isolated tools.

  • Understand which signals matter for each motion and ICP.
  • Connect enrichment and prioritization to ownership, routing and seller context.
  • Measure which signals and workflows contribute to pipeline and conversion.
Forecasting + revenue intelligence

One company view, different forecasting models.

Build each forecast around how that business actually generates revenue, then connect them into a company-level view of performance against plan, variance and what can still be influenced.

  • Use the forecast drivers, timing, seasonality and levels of certainty that fit each motion.
  • Make confidence gaps, true unknowns and the drivers behind variance visible.
  • Improve each model as the underlying operating model and data improve.
Rep + team workflow

Connect the architecture to the people doing the work.

For each change, understand what a rep, SME or partner-facing team needs to see, decide and do so the workflow supports the business outcome.

  • Reduce unnecessary steps, duplicate entry and context switching.
  • Put useful context at the point of action.
  • Be deliberate about what should be automated and where human judgment matters.
Automation + AI

Automate the work that should not stay manual.

Look across CRM hygiene, research, enrichment, meeting intelligence, pipeline summaries and cross-system reconciliation for repeatable work with a clear business case.

  • Separate process, context and data problems from tooling problems.
  • Build the smallest useful automation or AI workflow first.
  • Measure reclaimed capacity, decision quality or business impact before expanding.
How I would start

30 / 60 / 90: Understand → Build → Measure

Get to a useful current-state view quickly, improve what is clear, and use evidence from the first changes to shape what comes next.

Days 1–30

Understand + map

Learn how each business creates revenue and how the work moves today.

  • Map SPORT, EVENT and University Athlete from buyer and revenue model through lifecycle, systems, data, handoffs, workflow and reporting.
  • Map each business from plan → seasonal demand → pipeline creation → sales motion → revenue, then identify where performance is actually breaking down. A pipeline gap means something different if the business is entering its primary buying window than if the opportunity to create that pipeline was several months earlier.
  • Baseline the measures that matter for each motion, including forecast reliability, pipeline or demand health, velocity and CRM / reporting reliability.
  • Fix high-confidence issues as they surface.
Days 31–60

Build + test

Turn the map and baseline into concrete operating decisions and visible improvements.

  • Align with Travis and JL on the company-level view and the definitions or decision points that need to connect across businesses.
  • Stand up a first useful leadership view that brings the three businesses together without forcing the same forecast methodology across them.
  • Fix one or two meaningful funnel, handoff, workflow or data problems.
  • Test automation or AI against observed manual work with a clear business outcome.
Days 61–90

Measure + scale

Measure what changed and use the evidence to set the next priorities.

  • Operationalize the first forecast, pipeline and cross-functional cadences around the rhythms of each business.
  • Measure the business outcome alongside reliability, capacity, conversion, velocity and use.
  • Adjust the operating model and workflow based on what the team and data show.
  • Build the 6–12 month roadmap for data, systems, automation and resourcing.
